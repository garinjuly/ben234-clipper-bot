How it works

Three simple steps, from clip to published video.

1

The owner creates a clip

The owner creates short clips from campaign footage, ready to publish.

2

The bot uploads it

The bot uploads the clip through TikTok’s official Content Posting API, using the owner’s authorization.

3

Review & post

The owner reviews the video, and it is posted to his TikTok account, @ben234924.

Privacy

This app only accesses the owner’s own TikTok account, with his authorization. It is not a service for other users, and it is not offered to the public.

For the full details, please read the Privacy Policy.

  • Only the owner’s own account — no access to other users’ accounts.
  • Does not collect, store, or share data from other users.
  • No ads, and no selling of data.
  • Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok settings.
