7 Revoking access

The owner can revoke the App’s access at any time, for any reason, without contacting us first. Revoking access stops the App from publishing to the account.

To revoke access in TikTok:

  1. Open TikTok and go to Profile → Menu → Settings and privacy.
  2. Open Security & permissions → Apps and services (wording may vary slightly by app version).
  3. Select Ben234 Clipper Bot and remove its access.

Once access is revoked, the App can no longer post to @ben234924 unless the owner authorizes it again.
