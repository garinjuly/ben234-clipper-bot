Privacy Policy

How this personal tool handles information when it publishes the owner’s own short clips to the owner’s own TikTok account.

In short

  • ✓Only the owner’s TikTok account is accessed, and only after the owner authorizes it.
  • ✓No data about other TikTok users is collected, stored, or shared.
  • ✓No advertising, no tracking for ads, and no sale of data — ever.
  • ✓Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok Settings.

1 Overview

Ben234 Clipper Bot (“the App”, “we”, “our”) is a private, single-user tool built and operated by its owner. Its only purpose is to publish short video clips that belong to the owner to the owner’s own TikTok account, @ben234924, using TikTok’s official Content Posting API.

The App is not a public service. It is not offered to other creators, businesses, or the general public, and it does not operate accounts for anyone else.

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App accesses, why, and what it does not do. By authorizing the App in TikTok, the owner agrees to this policy.

2 Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to the sole user of the App: its owner and operator. Because the App is not open to the public, there are no other end users whose personal data is processed through it.

If you are not the owner, you do not need to — and cannot — sign in to or use this App, and the App does not collect information about you.

3 Information the App accesses

The App accesses only the minimum information needed to post a video via TikTok’s API, after explicit authorization by the owner through TikTok’s OAuth flow:

  • TikTok access token and open ID for the owner’s account (@ben234924) — provided by TikTok when the owner authorizes the App. This is used solely to authenticate API requests to post content.
  • Video files and captions the owner supplies — the clip files, titles, and descriptions the owner chooses to publish. These are sent to TikTok for posting.
  • Basic posting result returned by TikTok — for example, whether an upload succeeded and the resulting post identifier — so the owner can confirm the clip was published.

The App does not request or access the owner’s TikTok password. Authentication is handled entirely by TikTok.

4 How information is used

Information accessed by the App is used only for these purposes:

  • To authenticate the owner’s TikTok account with TikTok’s API.
  • To upload and publish the owner’s own clips to the owner’s own account.
  • To show the owner whether a post succeeded or failed.

There is no other use. The App does not use information for profiling, recommendations, research, or marketing.

5 What the App does not do

  • It does not collect, store, or share data about other TikTok users — including followers, viewers, commenters, or any third party.
  • It does not sell, rent, or trade any personal data to anyone.
  • It does not display advertising and does not include advertising trackers.
  • It does not read direct messages, scrape profiles, or download other people’s content.
  • It does not post, like, comment, or follow on behalf of anyone other than the owner, and only posts content the owner provides.

6 Storage and retention

The App is designed to store as little as possible:

  • Access tokens are kept only in the owner’s own private environment where the tool runs, solely so the owner does not have to re-authorize before every post.
  • Video files remain the owner’s files. The App passes them to TikTok for publishing; it does not build a separate public library or database of them.
  • When the owner revokes access or deletes a token, the App can no longer access the TikTok account, and any locally held token should be deleted.

No user database is maintained, because there are no other users.

7 Sharing of information

Information is shared only where it must go for the App to function:

  • With TikTok — video files, captions, and API requests are sent to TikTok to publish the post, under TikTok’s own terms and privacy policy.

Information is not shared with advertisers, data brokers, analytics providers, or any other third party. It will only be disclosed if required by law.

8 Cookies and tracking

This Privacy Policy page and the App do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking, and do not embed third-party analytics or ad technology. If this ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

9 Revoking access

The owner can revoke the App’s access at any time, without needing to contact us:

In the TikTok app: go to Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Apps and services (wording may vary by version), select Ben234 Clipper Bot, and remove its access. After revocation, the App immediately loses the ability to post to the account. Re-authorization is required to use it again.

The owner may also request deletion of any locally held tokens or logs by contacting us at the address in Section 13.

10 Security

Reasonable care is taken to protect the owner’s access token: it is not published, not shared, and not included in this page or in any public code. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so the owner should revoke access promptly if a device or file containing the token is lost or compromised.

11 Children

The App is a personal tool for its adult owner and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect information from children.

12 Changes to this policy

If how the App works changes in a way that affects privacy — for example, if new TikTok permissions are requested — this page will be updated and the “Last updated” date above will change. The current version always applies.
